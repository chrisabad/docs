AgentOS partitions every work stream into one of seven **Paperclip companies**. Each company is a hard isolation boundary — agents can only act on issues inside their assigned company, and routing rules, reviewers, and WIP limits are configured per company. The one exception is Juno, who holds CEO authority across all seven, and Reed, who dispatches work cross-company.

## Cross-company authority

Juno is CEO of every company. It can create issues, approve changes, and escalate in any company context. Reed is the dispatcher for all companies and manages the cross-company queue. No other agent has authority outside its assigned company.

## Routing rules

Each company's routing rules are defined in the issue-trigger plugin at `~/repos/paperclip-issue-trigger/routing-rules.json`. The rules map each company ID to:

- **Implementer agents** — who picks up and executes work
- **Reviewer agents** — who reviews completed issues before closing
- **Orchestrator** — always Juno
- **Dispatcher** — always Reed
- **WIP limits** — per-agent concurrency caps
