## Revenue tracking

Cass monitors LemonSqueezy continuously and sends alerts when:

- More than 3 cancellations occur in a single day
- Any single refund exceeds $50
- MRR changes materially

Financial data flows from LemonSqueezy to **Xero** for accounting reconciliation.
