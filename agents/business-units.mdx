Business-unit agents run the seven companies that make up the AgentOS portfolio. Each company has a dedicated team of agents — typically a CTO, CMO, and CFO — plus support, review, and chief-of-staff roles where needed. All business-unit agents report to Juno and communicate exclusively through Paperclip issue comments.
